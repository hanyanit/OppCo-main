# OppCo — Student Opportunities Platform

🌐 **Live Application:** https://oppco.co.za/

OppCo is a student opportunities platform designed to help students discover **bursaries they may qualify for** and **vacation work opportunities** that can help them gain valuable workplace experience.

The platform brings relevant student opportunities together in one place, making it easier for students to discover opportunities that can support both their **education and career development**.

---

## 🎯 What is OppCo?

Students often have to search across multiple websites, social media platforms, and organisation pages to find bursaries and work opportunities.

OppCo aims to simplify this process by providing a platform where students can discover opportunities that are relevant to them.

The platform focuses on two key areas:

- 🎓 **Bursary opportunities**
- 💼 **Vacation work opportunities**

By connecting students with relevant opportunities, OppCo helps students take the next step towards achieving their academic and career goals.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎓 Bursary Opportunities

OppCo helps students discover bursaries that they may qualify for based on their information and eligibility requirements.

This helps students spend less time searching through unrelated bursary listings and more time focusing on opportunities that may be suitable for them.

### 💼 Vacation Work

Students can discover vacation work opportunities that allow them to gain practical experience while studying.

Vacation work can help students:

- Gain real-world experience
- Develop professional skills
- Strengthen their CV
- Explore different industries
- Build professional connections

### 🔎 Opportunity Discovery

OppCo brings student opportunities together through a central platform, allowing students to explore available opportunities more conveniently.

## 🏗️ Technology Stack

OppCo uses a separate frontend and backend architecture.

### Frontend

The frontend was developed using:

- **Angular**
- **TypeScript**
- **HTML5**
- **CSS/SCSS**

Angular is responsible for the user interface and the interaction between students and the platform.

### Backend

The backend was developed using:

- **Python**
- Python-based backend APIs

The backend handles the application's server-side functionality and communication with the frontend.
