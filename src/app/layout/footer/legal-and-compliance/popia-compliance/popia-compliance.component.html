POPIA COMPLIANCE

1. Introduction

OppCo is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal data.

2. Purpose of Processing Personal Information

We process your personal information to provide you with personalised bursary and career opportunities, improve our services, and communicate important updates.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

OppCo processes your personal data based on your consent, legitimate interests, and where required, to fulfil contractual obligations or legal requirements.

4. Data Subject Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information held by OppCo.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
  • Request deletion or destruction of your personal data, where applicable.
  • Object to processing for direct marketing purposes.
  • Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against loss, theft, unauthorised access, disclosure, or alteration.

6. Data Retention

OppCo retains your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined or as required by law.

7. Sharing Personal Information

We do not sell your personal data. Personal information may be shared only with trusted third parties who assist us in providing services, or as required by law.

8. Contact Us

For any questions or to exercise your data subject rights under POPIA, please contact us at: legal@oppco.co.za
