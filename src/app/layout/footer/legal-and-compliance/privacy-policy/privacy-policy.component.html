PRIVACY POLICY

1. Information We Collect

OppCo collects and processes a variety of personal information in order to effectively match you with bursaries, vac work, and graduate programmes suited to your profile. This includes:

  • Contact & Profile Information: Full name, email address, phone number, profile photo, and biography.
  • Personal & Demographic Data: Date of birth, age, gender, race, nationality, disability status, and address (including district, city, municipality, province, and country).
  • Household Information: Annual household income, number of dependants, orphan status.
  • Academic Data: Current education level, grade, institution type and name, qualification details, modules/subjects, marks, study year, and academic averages.
  • User Preferences & Hobbies: Selected interests, hobbies, and responses related to field of study or aspirations.
  • Social Media Handles: Optional social media usernames (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter).
  • Technical Data: Browser type, device, operating system, IP address, and activity on our platform (e.g., page views, session duration).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Match You with Opportunities: Use your academic, demographic, and profile data to algorithmically identify and recommend funding, internships, and programmes that suit you.
  • Improve Platform Performance: Understand user behavior and improve the design and functionality of the platform.
  • Communicate with You: Send you relevant alerts, updates, support messages, and notifications about your matches or profile completeness.
  • Comply with Law: Fulfil obligations under applicable laws, including South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

3. Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data. However, as with all digital platforms, no system is fully immune to threats. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we do our best to protect your personal data at all times.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only with:

  • Trusted service providers: For hosting, support, and analytics.
  • Opportunity providers (bursars, universities, employers): Only when you apply or opt-in for sharing.
  • Legal authorities: If required by court orders, investigations, or statutory obligations.

5. Cookies

Our platform uses cookies to enhance usability, store your preferences, and improve matching accuracy. You may disable cookies via your browser settings, but this may affect your experience.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information.
  • Correction: Ask us to update inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request the removal of your profile and data.
  • Object: Withdraw from communications or data usage that you disagree with.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at legal@oppco.co.za.

7. Third-Party Links

Our platform may link to third-party websites or applications. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites, and encourage you to review their policies before sharing your information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You will be notified of major updates, and continued use of the platform implies your acceptance of any changes made.

9. Contact Us

For privacy concerns, support, or data-related requests, please contact us at: legal@oppco.co.za
