COOKIE POLICY

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

OppCo uses cookies to:

  • Enhance your user experience by remembering your preferences.
  • Analyze website traffic and usage to improve our platform.
  • Deliver personalized content and match you with relevant opportunities.
  • Ensure the security and proper functioning of our website.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function properly.
  • Performance Cookies: Collect information on how you use the site to help us improve performance.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings.
  • Advertising Cookies: Help deliver relevant ads and track ad performance.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality and performance of the OppCo platform.

5. Third-Party Cookies

OppCo may use trusted third-party services that set cookies on our site to provide analytics and advertising services. These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies.

6. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and your continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of such changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about our use of cookies, please contact us at: legal@oppco.co.za
